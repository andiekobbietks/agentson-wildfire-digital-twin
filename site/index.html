AgentSON Wildfire Digital Twin

A geographically grounded research scaffold connecting terrain and weather reconstruction, simulated aerial robotics, perception experiments, and portable AgentSON execution records.

Current status: design and research scaffold

This project is not an operational wildfire prediction or emergency-response system. Visual simulation is not physical validation, and simulated flight is not a real-world flight safety case.
